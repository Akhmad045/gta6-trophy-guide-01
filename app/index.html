    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough: there is no mission replay, no difficulty trophy and no online trophy, and free-roam continues after the credits.       Not missable, despite appearances

      Character questlines that go quiet mid-story (Kenton after Chapter 1, for one) all return before the end, and every quest giver is still around after the credits. Optional missions with hourglass timers can expire, but none of them feeds a trophy. The Kingpin Bowling valuables for Let’s Go Bowling can be collected across several visits. If you keep a save at each chapter start and take the Chapter 1 balcony meditation, the platinum cannot lock you out.
