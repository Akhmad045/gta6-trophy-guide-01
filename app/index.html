Grand Theft Auto VI · PlayStation 5 · Offline Trophy Guide

Leonida Nights

Every one of the 55 official trophies — name, description and grade as PlayStation publishes them — with a guide to each, a five-stage roadmap to the platinum, and a checklist that remembers your hunt.

Leonida Nights is an unofficial, fan-made guide. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list for Grand Theft Auto VI, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles (cross-checked 2026-11-18); tracked counts are PlayStation’s, via PSNProfiles. Every guide here is written for this guide from the official descriptions — where a description is cryptic, the guide says so instead of inventing steps. No Rockstar Games artwork or logos are used; the look is this guide’s own. Progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device except in a file you export yourself.
